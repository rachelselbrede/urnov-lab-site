CRISPR cures for children, on demand.

The Urnov Lab at the Innovative Genomics Institute designs and safety-tests personalized genome-editing therapies for children born with severe genetic diseases, then turns each one into a process that can be repeated for the next child.

Home of the Center for Pediatric CRISPR Cures, funded by a $20 million grant from the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative.

Co-developed the first personalized in vivo CRISPR therapy, designed, made, and delivered to a newborn in six months.

Part of the Innovative Genomics Institute, a joint effort of UC Berkeley, UCSF, and UC Davis founded by Jennifer Doudna.

What we work on

Most children with a rare genetic disease will never have a drug developed for them. Their conditions are too rare for the usual model. We are building a different one, where the tools, the tests, and the regulatory path are ready before the next patient arrives.

Personalized cures

The Center for Pediatric CRISPR Cures pairs cure design and safety testing at UC Berkeley with clinical care at UCSF. Its first goal is to treat eight children with severe inborn errors of immunity and metabolic diseases, and to share everything it learns with other academic centers.

Platform therapies

Through the Danaher-IGI Beacon for CRISPR Cures, we develop editing approaches that can treat a whole class of diseases at once, starting with severe Mendelian diseases of the immune system such as Artemis-deficient SCID.

Technology core

The CRISPR Cures Technology Core builds the readouts every program depends on, from guide RNA screening to deep sequencing of editing outcomes and genome-wide safety assessment.

Fyodor Urnov

Principal Investigator. Director of Therapeutic R&D at the IGI and Professor of Molecular Therapeutics at UC Berkeley

A genome-editing pioneer who helped build the human genome-editing toolbox and bring its first applications to the clinic. He directs the Center for Pediatric CRISPR Cures and the Danaher-IGI Beacon for CRISPR Cures, and was the IGI lead on the first personalized in vivo CRISPR therapy.
